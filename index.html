About DooMovie

DooMovie is your ultimate destination for discovering and exploring movies. Our platform is designed to help you find your next favorite film through personalized recommendations and comprehensive movie information.

Key Features

  • Smart Search — Find movies easily with our advanced search that delivers instant results as you type.
  • Curated Recommendations — Discover trending, top-rated, and upcoming movies across every genre.
  • Multiple Watchlists — Create custom lists to organize your movies by mood, occasion, or any category you like.
  • Mood Browsing — Pick your mood and discover movies that match how you're feeling right now.
  • Surprise Me — Can't decide? Let us pick a random movie for you with a fun roulette animation.
  • Indonesian Cinema Hub — Dedicated section celebrating the best of Indonesian filmmaking.
